Your LinkedIn, in seven steps

Ready images, a written About section, and a post you can put up today. Fifteen minutes, and a parent who looks you up finds a trained coach instead of a grey circle.

Add your profile photo

  • The Cuemath photo folder has ready profile and cover images you can use as they are.
  • Prefer your own photo? Use it. A clear picture of you works better than a logo — face a window, plain wall behind, head and shoulders.
  • On LinkedIn: Me → View profile, then the camera icon on the circle.

I am a math coach with X years of teaching behind me, and I coach students in Grades X to X at Cuemath. Every child can be good at math. Most of them just met it the wrong way first. I do not hand over answers. I ask the question that gets the child to the next step herself — that is what cueing means, and it is what makes the learning stick. Because I stay with the same students across years, I know exactly which topic each one quietly avoids. So far I have coached X students. One you are proud of — the girl who cried before fractions tests in March and explained them to her brother in August. If you are looking for a math coach for your child, message me here — happy to talk about where your child is right now.
Specific beats impressive. "140 students" and "the girl who cried before fractions tests" do more work than "passionate educator committed to excellence".
